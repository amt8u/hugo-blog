# Just a cheatsheet of google search

Put it in the title to get list of documents

```
intitle:"index of" "machine learning" pdf
intitle:"index of" ".pdf" site:.edu
intitle:"index of" "parent directory" thermodynamics
```

No space after the colon. 
Adding `-inurl:(htm|html|php)` trims pages that merely mention the phrase.

For finding papers and reports

```
filetype:pdf site:.gov.in "environmental impact assessment"
filetype:pdf "annual report" site:rbi.org.in after:2024
filetype:xlsx site:.edu "survey data"
```
